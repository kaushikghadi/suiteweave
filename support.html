Getting started

  1. Log in to NetSuite in a tab of the same browser.
  2. Click the SuiteWeave icon in the toolbar. The builder opens and shows "Connected".
  3. Choose the record your report starts from, add columns and filters, join related records, and press Run report.

No NetSuite at hand? Open SuiteWeave and choose Explore with demo data.

Common questions

"Not connected" or "Could not start NetSuite's query engine". Reload your NetSuite tab, check you are still logged in, then click the connection button at the top of SuiteWeave and choose Reconnect.

Does SuiteWeave change anything in NetSuite? No. It is read-only: it runs only SELECT queries with your own login and role, and installs nothing in NetSuite.

Ask AI says the API key is missing or rejected. Open Ask AI, then AI settings. Choose a service, paste your own key and press Test. Groq gives free keys at console.groq.com/keys; Google Gemini at aistudio.google.com/apikey.

Ask AI says the daily limit is reached. Free AI tiers allow a set number of requests per day. Try again later, or choose another service in AI settings.

Several NetSuite accounts. Open each account in its own tab. Click the connection button at the top of SuiteWeave to switch between them.
